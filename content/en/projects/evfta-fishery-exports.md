## The question

The EU–Vietnam Free Trade Agreement entered into force in August 2020, cutting tariffs on most Vietnamese seafood over several years. We asked whether the agreement had a measurable effect on fishery exports to the EU.

## The method

An econometric analysis of bilateral trade flows before and after the agreement, by product group, co-written with classmates at Foreign Trade University.

## Why it's here

It was my first piece of real empirical work: assembling the data, choosing a specification, and writing up a result that had to survive questions. The paper was selected for presentation at the Vietnam Economists Annual Meeting 2023.
