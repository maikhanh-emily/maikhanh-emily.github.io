## The brief

The Green Economy Forum & Exhibition (GEFE) is EuroCham Vietnam's annual meeting point between European and Vietnamese business on the green transition. Its 2024 business challenge asked student teams for a bankable green project. We chose offshore wind, because Vietnam has the wind resource and a national power plan that counts on it.

## My part

The commercial feasibility: a CAPEX and OPEX build-up from turbine, foundation, cable and installation costs, the levelised cost of electricity that follows, and scenarios across power price, capacity factor and the regulatory path for power purchase agreements. Then the investment case itself, presented to a panel of industry experts.

## What I took from it

The model is not the hard part. The assumptions are, and so is being able to say which three of them decide the answer. The scenario on regulatory timing moved the result more than any technical input, which says something about where risk really sits in energy projects in emerging markets.

Result: top 9 of more than 300 teams.
