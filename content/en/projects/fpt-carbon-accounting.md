## The setting

FPT IS is the technology-services arm of FPT, Vietnam's largest IT group. Its green transformation practice helps companies measure and report emissions as European rules, above all the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive, reach their supply chains.

## What I did

- Identified data fragmentation across entities as the main compliance risk, and coordinated the enterprise carbon-accounting rollout that closed it, across several client organisations at once and with no fixed playbook.
- Validated client disclosures against CSRD, GRI and IFRS S1/S2, and turned the findings into briefings and remediation plans for decision-makers.
- Synthesised the technical findings for cross-departmental workshops with 50+ stakeholders, translating regulatory obligations into language operational teams could act on.

## The lesson

The hardest part of carbon reporting isn't the climate science. It's making every number one you could defend to an auditor. A target you can't audit is a press release.
