## What it was

The Sports Club organises the university's tournaments, and tournaments cost money: venues, referees, prizes, printing. The External Relations Board exists to find it.

## What I did

Pitched the events to local businesses: who comes, what they see, what the sponsor gets. Closed about 20 million VND in cash sponsorship over the academic year 2021–22, enough to run the programme. The FTU Youth Union recognised the work with a certificate for outstanding results.

## Why it's here

It was my first sales job, before I knew that's what it was. Everything I've done since in business development, from cold outreach to funding applications, is the same motion: find out what the other side values, and make the ask in their terms.
