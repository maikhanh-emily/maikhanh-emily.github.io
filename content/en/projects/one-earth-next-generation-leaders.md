## The programme

One Earth Next Generation Leaders, run by Temasek Foundation, brings young people from across Asia-Pacific together for a week on sustainability, leadership and systems thinking. Participants are placed in mixed regional teams and given a real problem to work on.

## Our project

A circular food-production model: how to close the loops between farming, processing and waste so that a regional food system uses less land, water and energy. I led the quantitative side, sizing the flows, costing the loops and showing which ones pay for themselves.

## What stayed with me

Six people, six countries, one week. The quality of the work depended less on anyone's ideas than on how fast we could agree on what we were optimising for. That has been true of most teams I've been in since.
