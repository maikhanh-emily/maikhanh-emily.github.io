## The assignment

Pick a venture idea, then analyse the financing environment it would enter: 10 to 20 comparable companies, their funding rounds, investors, valuations and exits, and what that implies about the industry's stage and prospects.

## My half

Antibody-based cancer therapeutics, with antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) at the core. The venture idea is a virtual ADC company for patients whose tumours have stopped responding to today's leading ADCs, which share one payload class. The company would in-license proven components and own the clinical positioning.

The work: a longlist of ADC companies filtered and scored in a workbook, financing histories checked in PitchBook, and a source log so that every figure in the paper can be traced back.

## What's interesting

Recent ADC deals show pharma paying large sums upfront for preclinical platforms. For a seed-stage venture that changes the whole financing logic: the realistic exit is an acquisition before Phase 1, so the capital plan is built around reaching that point, not around an IPO.
