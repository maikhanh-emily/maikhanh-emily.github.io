I grew up in Vietnam and studied international business economics at Foreign Trade University in Hanoi, with an exchange semester in Erlangen, Germany. In 2025 I moved to Norway for a master's in finance at BI Norwegian Business School, splitting my time between Bergen and Oslo.

What I like is the point where analysis has to become a decision: a business case someone funds, a disclosure someone signs, a pipeline a team actually works from. At FPT IS I helped companies make their carbon numbers defensible. At Planck Technologies, a deep-tech start-up that designs materials for CO₂ capture, I ran customer outreach and public-funding applications, and built the two CRMs the team works from, with AI coding assistants and no engineering resource.

I care about things being true before they look finished. Most of what I write here is about that, one way or another.
